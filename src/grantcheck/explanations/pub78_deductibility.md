# Publication 78 deductibility

**What it checks.** Whether the organization appears in IRS Publication 78 Data, the
published list of organizations eligible to receive tax-deductible charitable
contributions.

**Why it matters.** Funders and pass-through entities use Publication 78 as the fast proof
of good standing. Being absent is something you want to be able to explain before somebody
else notices it.

**The trap this check exists to avoid.** A subordinate organization covered by another
organization's group ruling does **not** appear in Publication 78 in its own right. The
central organization is listed, and the ruling covers its subordinates. Roughly 238,000
organizations are in this position, and reporting their absence as a problem would tell a
compliant organization it cannot receive deductible contributions. The check identifies
them from the Business Master File and reports "not applicable" instead.

**What to do.** If you are a group subordinate, a funder asking for proof of deductibility
wants the central organization's group exemption letter, not a Publication 78 entry. If you
are not, and you are absent, the common causes are a recent recognition that has not
propagated yet, a revoked exemption, or being a church — which may receive deductible
contributions without being listed.
