# Exempt status

**What it checks.** Whether the Internal Revenue Service records this organization as
recognized under section 501(c)(3) of the tax code, with an unconditional exemption.

**Why it can stop an application.** Most federal grant programs limit eligibility to
organizations described in section 501(c)(3). If the IRS Exempt Organizations Business
Master File does not show that, the applicant is not what the notice of funding opportunity
says it must be, and a reviewer will find out before you do.

**What "unknown" means here.** Absence from the Business Master File is not a finding
against an organization. Churches and their integrated auxiliaries are exempt without
applying and are largely absent from it, as are government instrumentalities and
organizations recognized very recently. The check says so rather than guessing.

**What to do.** Confirm against the IRS Tax Exempt Organization Search at
<https://apps.irs.gov/app/eos/>. If the subsection is not 03, read the eligibility section
of the specific notice — some programs do accept other subsections.
