# Registration expiration

**What it checks.** The expiration date on the SAM.gov entity registration, and how far away
it is.

**Why an active registration can still be a problem.** SAM.gov registration lapses annually
and renewal is **not** automatic. A registration showing "Active" today that expires in
three weeks is the thing that quietly disqualifies an application submitted next month. The
check warns inside 60 days, which is roughly four times the ten to fifteen business days a
renewal takes to take effect.

**Expired is not the same as missing.** They are different problems with different
timelines: renewing is ten to fifteen business days, registering for the first time is
weeks. The check keeps them separate.

**What to do.** Renew at <https://sam.gov>, and do it before the deadline rather than at it.
