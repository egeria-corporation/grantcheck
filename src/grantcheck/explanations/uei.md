# Unique Entity ID

**What it checks.** Whether the organization has a Unique Entity ID, the twelve-character
identifier that replaced the DUNS number as the federal government's way of identifying
entities it does business with.

**Why it can stop an application.** No Unique Entity ID means no SAM.gov registration, which
means no federal award. Every federal application will ask for it.

**What to do.** A Unique Entity ID is requested through <https://sam.gov> and is free. It is
the first step of registering, so an organization that has one has at least started.
