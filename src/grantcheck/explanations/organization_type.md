# Organization type

**What it checks.** Whether the IRS classifies this organization as a private foundation,
using the foundation code in the Business Master File.

**Why it matters.** Most federal grant programs restrict eligibility to public charities
and exclude private foundations. This surprises more people than it should, and it is
better established before anyone invests forty hours in an application.

**Why it is a warning rather than a failure.** It is a classification, not a
disqualification. Some programs do accept private foundations. The check reports the fact
and points you at the eligibility section of the specific notice of funding opportunity.

**What to do.** If the classification looks wrong, it comes from the IRS determination and
is corrected with the IRS, not with the funder.
